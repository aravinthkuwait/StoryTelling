**TTS notes:** Tamil neural TTS (e.g. Google `ta‑IN`, ElevenLabs multilingual, Azure
`ta‑IN` Pallavi/Valluvar) handles these lines well. Piper (zero‑key, bundled with
OpenMontage) has limited/*no* Tamil voice — if Piper is the only option, use it for an
English‑subtitle scratch track and source Tamil VO from a Tamil‑capable engine or a human
voice actor. Normalize all VO to ‑16 LUFS; dialogue ‑14 LUFS; duck music under speech by ~9 dB.

## MUSIC SCORE MAP

Style brief: **magical orchestral score blended with soft Tamil folk instruments**
(flute/venu, nadaswaram accents, veena, tabla/mridangam, strings), emotional and
adventurous, with an inspiring finale. Single thematic motif ("Kindness theme")
introduced softly at open, fully resolved at the end.

## Mix targets

- VO: ‑16 LUFS, centered. Dialogue: ‑14 LUFS, follow on‑screen position lightly.
- Music bed: ‑23 LUFS, duck ‑9 dB under speech. SFX: tasteful, ‑18 to ‑12 LUFS.
- Master: ‑14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ ‑1 dBTP (web delivery).
