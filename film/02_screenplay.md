## SCENE 2 — FAMILY OUTING (0:35–1:00)

*Nandini proposes a day out. Aravinth resists, then relents. A joyful road trip.*

**S2.1 — (0:35–0:41) Nandini's proposal.**
Nandini sits beside Aravinth, gently closing his laptop a little, hopeful.
> **NANDINI (TA):** இன்று ஒரு நாள் வேலை இல்லாமல் குடும்பத்தோட வெளிய போவோமா?
> **NANDINI (TR):** Iṉṟu oru nāḷ vēlai illāmal kuṭumbattōṭa veḷiya pōvōmā?
> **NANDINI (EN):** Just for one day — no work — shall we go out together as a family?

**S2.2 — (0:41–0:46) Aravinth refuses.**
Aravinth hesitates, frowning at his screen, shaking his head.
> **ARAVINTH (TA):** இப்போ வேலை ரொம்ப இருக்கு நந்தினி...
> **ARAVINTH (TR):** Ippō vēlai romba irukku Nandini...
> **ARAVINTH (EN):** There's so much work right now, Nandini...
The children's faces fall — Mirthula and Hamruthaa look down, disappointed.

**S2.3 — (0:46–0:51) He relents.**
He sees Hamruthaa's hopeful, sad eyes. His expression softens; he closes the laptop
and smiles for the first time.
> **ARAVINTH (TA):** சரி... இன்னைக்கு வேலை வேண்டாம். போகலாம்!
> **ARAVINTH (TR):** Sari... iṉṉaikku vēlai vēṇṭām. Pōkalām!
> **ARAVINTH (EN):** Alright... no work today. Let's go!
The children cheer and jump.

**S2.4 — (0:51–0:56) Leaving for the picnic.**
The family piles into a small car with a picnic basket; the monkey toy peeks from
Hamruthaa's bag. Smiles all around.
*(Happy music swells — no dialogue.)*

**S2.5 — (0:56–1:00) Road‑trip drone montage.**
Drone shot: the car winds through bright‑green fields and palm avenues under a blue

*(Upbeat Tamil folk‑orchestral music — no dialogue.)*

## SCENE 4 — THE GENIE ARRIVES (1:35–2:00)

**S4.3 — (1:49–1:56) The Genie offers three wishes.**

> **GENIE (TA):** உங்களுக்கு மூணு ஆசைகள் தர்றேன். யோசிச்சு கேளுங்க!
> **GENIE (TR):** Uṅgaḷukku mūṇu āsaigaḷ tarrēṉ. Yōsichchu kēḷuṅga!

**S4.4 — (1:56–2:00) The family exchanges excited looks.**
