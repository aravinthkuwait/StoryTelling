## 1. FINAL SCREENPLAY

```
FADE IN.

BLACK. Two seconds. Silence gives way to the faintest room tone —
a ceiling fan, breathing walls of an ordinary apartment at night.

INT. ARJUN'S BEDROOM - NIGHT

An extreme close-up: a digital clock. 3:12 AM. Dust catches a sliver
of reflected light. The clock ticks to 3:13. On the exact change —
CLICK. A door latch moves somewhere in the dark.

Arjun's eyes open. Not violently. Pupils adjusting.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
சில கதவுகள்... நாம திறக்கக்கூடாது.
(pause)
சில கதவுகள்... தானாகவே திறக்கும்.

Arjun sits up. Bedsheet drags. He breathes, listens. The door —
ajar 20cm, wood friction still settling. A dark corridor beyond.
He holds. Nothing.

ARJUN
(low, barely voiced)
யாரு...?

No answer. He waits two more seconds before he stands, barefoot,
and follows the doorway into an empty corridor.

MONTAGE - THREE NIGHTS

Night one: 3:13. CLICK. The door opens. Night two: 3:13. CLICK.
It opens wider. Night three: Arjun is already sitting up, awake,
tired, watching the clock hit 3:12.

NARRATOR (V.O.)
மூணு நாளா... சரியா 3:13க்கு... அதே சத்தம். அதே கதவு.
(longer pause)
ஆனா... வெளியே யாருமே இல்லை.

INT. ARJUN'S BEDROOM - NIGHT (FOURTH NIGHT)

Arjun sits awake. The clock counts: 3:12:56... 57... 58... 59.
The fan sound thins. The city outside goes quiet. By 3:12:59 the
room is close to silent.

3:13. CLICK. The door opens. From behind Arjun, down the far end
of the corridor — MAYA. Perfectly still.

No music sting.

Arjun's eyes widen. His jaw tightens. His breath stops — then,
after a second, a small involuntary inhale.

ARJUN
(whisper)
யார்... நீ?

Maya does not answer.

The camera creeps toward her, agonizingly slow. Her face enters
better light — hair strands, skin, eyes, saree weave, an earring
catching a glint. She is beautiful. Not horrifying. She does not
blink.

ARJUN
உன்னை... நான் எங்கேயோ பார்த்திருக்கேன்...

The faintest smile crosses her face. Close on her eyes.

MAYA
(distant whisper)
அர்ஜுன்...

He turns quickly to check behind him — nobody. He turns back.
Maya is closer. No movement was shown. A subtle low-frequency
pressure enters the air.

Arjun's breathing quickens. Fragments intrude — rain, headlights,
a wet road, glass, Maya's eyes, his own hand on the asphalt, a red
tail light receding. Each flash carries its own small sound: rain
hit, a muffled horn, a breath, glass — never chaotic.

ARJUN
இல்ல... இது... உண்மை இல்ல.

MAYA
(soft)
நான் உன்னை கூட்டிட்டுப் போக வரல...
(pause)
நீ தான்...
(pause)
இங்கிருந்து வரணும்.

The music all but disappears. Her voice is the only score.

INT. ARJUN'S BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS

Arjun looks around the room. The fan turns — no sound. The curtain
moves — no air sound. He touches the wall; for a fraction of a
second there is no tactile sound at all. Wrong. He turns toward
the mirror.

MAYA (O.S.)
நேரமாச்சு...

The clock: 3:13.

ARJUN
(quietly)
என்ன நேரம்?

Maya extends her hand, slowly. A long pause. Her expression is sad.

MAYA
நீ இறந்து...

ONE SECOND OF SILENCE.

Cut to Arjun. His eyes fill.

MAYA (O.S.)
நாலு நாள் ஆச்சு.

COMPLETE SILENCE. Hold on his face. No music for a beat or two —
this is the strongest moment in the film.

A soft, low emotional tone returns. Fear drains from Arjun's face;
sadness replaces it. A small tremble in his lower lip. One tear,
no performance of crying. Maya's expression is compassionate. He
looks toward the bed — camera blocks the direct view — then walks
to her. Soft footsteps. He places his hand in hers. No romantic
cue. Acceptance, nothing more.

They walk together, away from camera, deeper into the bedroom.
Saree cloth, bare feet, room tone. In the corridor behind them, one
light switches off. Then another. Then another. Unhurried.

They are gone. The door begins to close.

CLICK.

The bedroom, empty, for one second. Only electrical ambience.

The camera pans, very slowly, to the mirror. It reveals: Arjun's
body, motionless, on the bed. Same face. Same clothes. No gore.
Beside him, the clock: 3:13 AM. The reflection is geometrically
correct — no impossible angle.

No music. Only a faint electrical hum.

CUT TO BLACK. Absolute silence.

MAYA (V.O.)
(a whisper, very close)
இப்போதாவது... வந்துட்டியே.

A small breath. Then — nothing. All audio cut.

[optional, in edit only, +2-3s]
BLACK CARD, WHITE TAMIL TEXT, NO MUSIC:
"நாம் இழந்தவர்களை மறக்க முடியாது...
ஆனால் சில நேரங்களில் அவர்களை விடுவிக்க கற்றுக்கொள்ள வேண்டும்."

FADE OUT.
```

## 7. LIGHTING BIBLE

- **Base look:** cool neutral moonlight key, subtle warm corridor practical as secondary/motivated source, slightly desaturated shadows, natural skin tone, controlled highlights, deep-but-detailed blacks.
- **No equal illumination** — faces partially move in and out of shadow across a shot.

## 9. FACIAL EXPRESSION & MICRO-EXPRESSION DIRECTION (per key beat)

Every prompt for a shot containing a character specifies, in this order: eye direction → eyebrow tension → jaw tension → lips → breath → head angle → posture/shoulders → emotional transition. Bodies never move as a first reaction — eyes react first.

- **S1-C (eyes open):** pupils contract slightly as they adjust to dark; no brow movement yet; lips closed and relaxed; breath shallow and slow; head does not move.
- **S4-D (first sight of Maya):** eyes widen fractionally, lower eyelids tighten, brow draws in faint concern (not full fear), lips part slightly, jaw stiffens, breath visibly stops for ~1s then one small audible inhale through the nose, head stays still, shoulders rise a fraction only after the breath — never before.
- **S5-C (recognition):** eyes narrow slightly in recall, brow softens from fear toward confusion, head tilts a few degrees, posture leans forward an inch.
- **S6-C (denial):** eyes dart, brief blink rate increase, jaw trembles almost imperceptibly on "இல்ல", shoulders pull back slightly (retreat instinct) while feet stay planted.
- **S7-G (grief onset):** lower eyelids fill with liquid line first, no eyebrow spike, lips press together, one slow blink squeezes a single tear, breath held then released unevenly.
- **S8-A (acceptance):** brow fully relaxes, eyes soften and stay wet but no further tears, tiny lower-lip tremble then stillness, one slow nod substitutes for dialogue.
- **Maya, throughout:** rare blinking, held eye contact, minimal head movement, faint closed-mouth smile only at S5-B/S5-D, calm stillness is the horror engine — no wide eyes, no smiling teeth, no head tilt tricks.

## 19. LIP-SYNC STRATEGY

1. Generate each dialogue video shot with `generate_audio: true` off where a separate high-quality TTS track will replace it, OR use Seedance 2.0's native audio only for ambient/non-dialogue shots.
2. For every line-carrying shot: generate the Tamil TTS line first (Text to Speech V2 / Seed Audio, locked voice per character), measure its exact duration, then generate the video shot with a matching spoken-line duration budget so the mouth performance window lines up.
3. In FFmpeg assembly, replace/overlay the clean TTS dialogue track on top of the shot's video at the precise timecode from §17, ducking any native ambient/video audio under it (see §21 automated duck step).
4. Never manually reflow or time-stretch a TTS line to fit video — regenerate the video shot to the audio's real duration instead, to keep natural cadence.

## 20. CLIP CONTINUITY INSTRUCTIONS

- Every bedroom shot must be generated with the `Bedroom-Master` element as an image reference; every corridor shot with `Corridor-Master`. This is what prevents "furniture drift" between clips.
- Arjun always uses `Aravinth-v2`; Maya always uses `Maya-Lock` (see §3) — one element ID per character for the whole film, no exceptions.

- J-cuts: the door's CLICK begins a fraction of a second before its visual cut. L-cuts: Maya's last word may bleed a beat into Arjun's reaction shot. Both implemented in the FFmpeg audio timeline, not baked into individual clip renders.

## 27. GOOGLE COLAB / FFMPEG ASSEMBLY

See `colab/assemble_the_door_that_opens_at_313am.ipynb` (companion file in this same folder, complete and ready to run in Google Colab) for the full runnable pipeline: clip normalization → ambience bed construction → dialogue/SFX/music layering → ducking → loudness/limiting → final mux → automated QC checks. Summary of stages:

1. Ingest all Higgsfield clip exports + all audio stems (dialogue WAVs, score stems, Foley, ambience beds) into `/content/assets/`.
2. `ffprobe` every video clip — log resolution, fps, duration, pixel format.
3. Normalize: scale/pad to 1920x1080, convert to 24fps (no frame-blend interpolation — nearest/duplicate only), force `yuv420p`.
4. Trim any frozen trailing frames per §21's frame-match log.
5. Concatenate clips via `ffmpeg concat` demuxer into the master silent-video timeline, in shot order from §1.
6. Build continuous Layer-A/Layer-B ambience beds as standalone WAV stems spanning full scene ranges (bedroom bed, corridor bed).
7. Lay dialogue WAVs at exact timecodes from §17; lay Foley/door-signature/3:13-motif/Maya-presence stems at their sync points from §11–§14; lay score per the dynamic map in §16.
8. Duck ambience+score 4–8dB under every dialogue line (sidechain-style gain automation).
9. Apply the two absolute-silence gaps (§15) as literal gain-to-zero windows on every stem except the one required line.
10. Mix to stereo, gentle EQ, light dialogue compression, true-peak limiter; target **-14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP**, 48kHz.
11. Mux final audio to the final video timeline as `The_Door_That_Opens_at_3_13_AM_Final.mp4`.
12. Automated QC: verify total duration ≈180s (±1s), no zero-byte/broken frames, no unintended silent gaps outside the three designed silence windows, and audio/video sync drift < 1 frame at 5 spot-checked cut points.
