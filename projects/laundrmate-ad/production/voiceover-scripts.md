# Laundrmate Ad — Voiceover & Subtitle Scripts (Tamil / English / Hindi)

One master visual video; three audio/subtitle versions. Only voiceover and
subtitles change between versions.

**Tone:** natural, modern, warm-professional. Tamil must sound natural and
modern for Chennai customers — no old-fashioned or overly formal Tamil. Keep
English service terms as-is in all languages: App, WhatsApp, Pickup, Delivery,
Washing, Dry Cleaning, Steam Ironing, Quality Check.

**Phone number read-out:** "98847 12121" — spoken clearly, digit-grouped
(9-8-8-4-7, 1-2-1-2-1).

  English/Hindi lines are direct natural translations of the locked Tamil VO.
- Scene 13 VO may start over the Scene 12→13 transition so the number lands
  fully on screen; keep total runtime ≈ 60s.
- Subtitles per language: same text as VO, bottom-centred, never covering the
  offer card text or phone number.
- Output audio: one clean VO track per language (`vo_tamil.wav`,
  `vo_english.wav`, `vo_hindi.wav`) + one shared music/SFX bed
  (`music_bed.wav`), mixed in the Colab notebook.
