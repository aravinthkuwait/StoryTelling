## Flow and continuity rules

One connected story:

Shop opening → family laundry need → app booking → pickup → delivery bike
travelling → shop receiving → sorting → washing → drying → dry cleaning →
ironing → quality check → packing → delivery → happy customer → final
call-to-action.

- Use the **same garment bag** throughout pickup and shop receiving.
- Use the **same tagged school uniform, office shirt and saree** throughout.
- Use the **same customer family and delivery executive**.
- Use the **same shop interior** throughout.
- Consistent daylight: **morning** (opening + pickup), **midday** (processing),
  **evening / late afternoon** (delivery).
- Do not jump randomly between locations.
- Do not show duplicate people. Do not change uniforms.
- Do not change the storefront banner. Do not change the phone number.
- Do not create unrelated machinery.
- Do not generate disconnected montage clips without a visible story connection.

## Editing requirements

Fast and energetic but easy to understand. **Average shot length: 1.5–3 seconds.**

Quick close-ups for: machine controls, washing drum, dryer, steam iron, order
tag, packing, phone booking, delivery handover.

Wider shots for: shop opening, customer home, laundry interior, delivery
arrival, final storefront.

Natural match cuts:

- App confirmation → delivery bike
- Laundry bag at home → laundry bag at shop
- Washing drum → dryer drum
- Dryer door → ironing board
- Folded shirt → packed shirt
- Packed bag at shop → delivered bag at home

Use motion continuation between clips.

**Do NOT include:** frozen frames; sudden black frames; unmatched camera
direction; random 3D objects; unrealistic floating clothes; clothes entering
machines by themselves; staff standing idle without purpose; water or foam
outside machines; machines with distorted doors or controls; dry clothes
becoming wet again; wet clothes going directly for packing; shoes inside a
normal garment washing machine; customer orders getting mixed; excessive slow
motion; overly long logo scenes.
